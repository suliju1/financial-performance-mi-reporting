# Financial Performance & MI Reporting Solution

End-to-end Finance Data and Management Information reporting Project designed to simulate a real-world Finance Systems / FP&A reporting environment.

## Business Scenario

Aurelia Technologies Ltd is a UK-based B2B SaaS company operating across the UK and Europe.

The project addresses a fragmented and manual monthly management reporting process by integrating actual, budget and master data into a scalable financial reporting solution.
